1. SEO Optimization
Clean, semantic HTML structure (proper heading hierarchy, meta tags, alt text)

Fast page load speed (optimized images, minimal render-blocking scripts)

Mobile-first indexing compliance
Schema markup (structured data) for business info, reviews, products/services

XML sitemap and robots.txt
Keyword-optimized page titles, meta descriptions, and content
Local SEO setup (Google Business Profile integration, NAP consistency) if relevant

2.Responsive & User-Friendly Design
Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop

Intuitive navigation with clear calls-to-action (CTA)
Accessible design (WCAG basics: contrast, alt text, keyboard navigation)

Fast, lightweight, no unnecessary bloat
Consistent branding (colors, fonts, logo placement)

3. Business Promotion
Clear homepage messaging: what you do, who it's for, why choose you

Services/products page with details and pricing (if applicable)
About page building trust/credibility

Testimonials or case studies section

Contact page with form, map, phone/email, and social links

Strong CTAs throughout (e.g., "Get a Quote", "Book Now", "Contact Us")
